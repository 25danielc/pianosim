## Render estimate (measured on this Mac: Apple M5, 10-core GPU, Metal)

One **open-gate** render at 1920×1350 per frame; the 16:9 (1920×1080) and 4:5
(1080×1350) versions are both native-pixel crops of it, with the crop window set
per shot. That is ≈27% fewer pixels than two separate renders, and the
animation is identical in both.

Stand-in scene benchmark (lacquer, 88 keys + 88 hammers, 230 strings,
volumetric spot beam, windows, DOF, motion blur), per frame at 1920×1350:

| engine | settings | wide | macro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cycles | 256 spp, adaptive 0.02, OIDN | 28 s | 27 s |
| Cycles | 96 spp, adaptive 0.04, OIDN | 16 s | 20 s |
| EEVEE | 64 samples, raytracing, volumetrics | 2.8 s | — |

The real scene (modelled piano, hall, seats, curtains, rain glass) will cost about
2× the stand-in, giving these estimates for 2,730 frames:

| plan | what | est. |
|---|---|---|
| A (spec) | all Cycles, final quality | ≈ 40 h |
| **B (recommended)** | Cycles for the 7 macro inserts + the come-apart (shots 4, 10, 16, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25, 32 ≈ 525 frames); EEVEE for everything else | **≈ 9 h** |
| C (under 6 h) | Cycles for the 7 macro inserts only (≈ 310 frames, 64 spp); EEVEE for everything else | ≈ 5.5–6 h |

I'll re-measure on the real scene during look-dev (step 2) and update these.
