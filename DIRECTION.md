# Moonlight — III. Presto agitato · art direction

**Concept:** a black concert grand alone in an empty hall at night. Everything
is still except the keys: the music is the only energy in the frame.
Mood: cold, nocturnal, restless underneath. Recurring idea: moonlight from one
tall window falling across lacquer and ivory.

## Palette (no other colours)
| role | hex |
|---|---|
| background / void | `#07090F` |
| hall, floor, shadow fill | `#0E1A2E` |
| moonlight key light | `#CFE0FF` |
| ivory keys | `#F2EDE3` |
| ebony keys, piano body | `#0B0B0D` |
| **only accent:** pressed-key glow | `#8FB4FF` |

The piano interior (normally gold plate, felt, copper strings) is rendered in
palette: graphite plate and dark soundboard, with strings as faint silver.

## Type
Cormorant Garamond only (local OFL font files). Light/regular weights, generous
tracking on small caps, no trailing periods.

## Light & finish
Single cool key light from an upper-left window (spotlight with a window-pane
cookie), soft volumetric shafts, slow dust motes inside the beam, glossy
clearcoat lacquer reflecting an in-palette environment. ACES filmic tonemap,
sRGB, gentle vignette, very soft bloom limited to lit keys.

## Camera language
One continuous take. Slow push-ins and drifts, sine in-out, every move ≥ 6 s,
no cuts, no shake. 16:9 1920×1080 @ 60 fps.

## Motion rules
Keys: rotate down up to 3.5° about the rear pivot (depth ∝ velocity), full
depth on the onset frame, spring back on note-off. Glow ∝ velocity; held or
pedalled notes stay lit and fade while the pedal holds them.
Haze, dust and camera all move slowly. Nothing flickers, pops or jitters:
no per-frame noise, no film grain.

## Banned
Shockwave rings, particle bursts, chromatic aberration, camera shake, lens
flares, neon, extra hues, hard cuts, bouncy easing, falling-note "Synthesia"
bars, on-screen text during the music.
